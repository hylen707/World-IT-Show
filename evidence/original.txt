Company Research Report: Graphene Square Inc.
1. Overview of Graphene: The Dream Material
Graphene is a 2D nanomaterial with a single-layer honeycomb structure of carbon atoms, discovered in 2004. It is widely recognized as a pivotal foundational material poised to drive disruption and innovation across modern industrial sectors.

	3. Core Technology
Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD)
* Industrial Mass Production: Developed the world’s first roll-to-roll continuous large-area CVD production methodology.
* Synthesis Precision: Decomposes gaseous hydrocarbon precursors inside high-temperature thermal chambers to synthesize pristine, defect-free monolayer graphene at scale.
* Commercial Scalability: Establishes a reproducible manufacturing pipeline that successfully bridges the historical gap between laboratory synthesis and full-scale industrial supply.
4. Key Products
Graphene Multi-Cooker
* Premium culinary device powered by proprietary planar graphene surface heating elements.
* Ensures rapid, edge-to-edge heat distribution with superior energy efficiency compared to legacy resistive coils.
Graphene Radiator
* State-of-the-art space heating unit generating clean radiant warmth without open flames, convection blowers, or visible coils.
* Operates with an exterior surface safety ceiling of 80°C to eliminate burn hazards.
* Leverages high optical transparency to project customized holographic display visuals directly onto the emitter glass.
