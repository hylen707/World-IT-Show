## Updating evidence

Preserve S01 bytes; add a new revision as a new source if the original changes. Add concise paraphrases with exact locations; keep manufacturer assertions distinct from independent results. Do not infer attendance, dates, hours, or authorship from a linked document. Student records must be independently located and linked before personal claims are used.

No non-text images were captured by the export. Preserve minimal evidence and stable locators without unnecessarily republishing third-party material or personal data. The validator checks source hashes and ledger consistency, not the truth of every source assertion.
