# Research design decisions

## 01 · Company descriptions retain a source boundary

Company cases identify what a source reports, the business question under review, and the claims that matter. Unknown commercial metrics remain null. A vendor page can establish that the vendor makes a statement; it cannot independently verify revenue, deployment or system performance.

## 02 · Technology judgments are research actions

The disposition vocabulary is investigate, design_test, replicate and pause. It is separate from evidence stage: reported, literature_checked, synthetic_only or fixture_measured. No production-readiness score or imported radar ring is assigned. Each action requires a next question, a required record and an acceptance condition.

## 03 · Method references are inspectable

The benchmark register pins the inspected upstream commit, file, line range, blob identity, license context and local adoption path. The approach transfers a practice, not institutional authority. No upstream code or branding is incorporated.

## 04 · Reproduction and documentation form one contract

The complete offline verification checks original source integrity, regenerated outputs, research references, artifact hashes, documentation links and tests. Generated README counts and the research status derive from the same records. A changed hash requires deliberate review and refresh; it does not by itself imply a scientific change.

The existing detailed provenance records remain intact. Technical verification does not authenticate personal contribution or turn a proposed experiment into completed work.
