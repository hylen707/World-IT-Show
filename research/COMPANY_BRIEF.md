# Graphene Square: the evidence behind a device advantage

## Research proposition

The supplied report describes an appealing route from CVD graphene to heating appliances. A defensible company assessment must connect three distinct propositions: a reproducible film, a useful device advantage, and evidence that customers can obtain that advantage under defined conditions. The present research establishes questions and technical boundaries; it does not establish financial value or market leadership.

The machine-readable [company case](entities.json) attributes offerings to S01, S05 and S06. Legal identity, revenue, funding and customer deployments have not been independently established here. Their absence from this dataset does not imply absence in the business.

| Research layer | What the inspected record supports | What remains necessary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | A company report and primary-paper checks identify relevant graphene properties and an area-convention discrepancy | Match material type, face convention and mass basis before comparing numbers |
| Manufacturing | The report names CVD; inspected literature shows why process labels alone cannot define lot quality | Process version, spatial quality sampling, acceptance criteria and yield |
| Device benefit | Manufacturer descriptions identify heating products; the analytical model exposes task-energy dependencies | Matched load and endpoint, wall-energy measurements, repeats and uncertainty |
| Commercial delivery | The original report describes expansion strategy | Dated product availability and independently attributable customer/deployment records |
| Defensibility | Priority is asserted in the supplied report | Exact research advance, inventorship, licensing and company lineage records |

## Competing explanations

A faster heater can improve convenience without reducing energy per completed task. A transparent heating surface can provide a design benefit even when a broad energy claim remains unproven. Earlier research by founders may explain pre-incorporation lineage. A contemporary product page may concern another version. These alternatives prevent an unsupported negative verdict while identifying the evidence that a stronger positive case needs.

## Decision consequence

The next useful action is a bounded comparison, not a company ranking. Hold the useful task and comparator conditions fixed, preserve unsuccessful runs, and record the surface or product version under discussion. Separate technical benefit from market adoption and financial performance.
